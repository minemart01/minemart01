Local Product Images Directory
===============================

You can save your product photos in this folder (e.g. `shirt.jpg`, `dress.png`).

To use them in `src/data/products.ts`, simply set:
  image: "/products/shirt.jpg"

Vite automatically serves everything in this `public` folder directly from the website root.
You can also use full online URLs:
  image: "https://example.com/photo.jpg"
